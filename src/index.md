      <p>Hi, I'm Reverie.</p>

      <p>I'm an artist, craftsperson, and creative technology educator based in Brooklyn, New York.</p>

      <p>I hold a B.F.A. in Art and Technology from The Ohio State University and an M.A. in Interactive Media Arts from NYU's Tisch School of the Arts.</p>

      <p>I'm currently a Post-Doctoral Fellow with NYU Tisch, where I experiment with unexpected connections across artistic mediums through play, movement, and making.</p>

      <p>When I'm not in the studio or classroom, I coach and choreograph artistic swimming and teach third graders how to code, spending a lot of time thinking about how people learn, move, and stay curious.</p>
